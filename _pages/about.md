Welcome! I am a research scientist at MIT Energy Initiative. My research interests lie at the intersection of machine learning and optimization for secure operation in renewable rich power grids. Till mid-2024, I was a Staff Scientist in the Theoretical Division at Los Alamos National Laboratory, a US DOE lab, where I served as a PI/co-PI for projects on physics-informed machine learning in energy systems. I had moved to Los Alamos in November 2015 to do a post-doc in the Cener for Non-Linear Studies (CNLS) after my PhD in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Texas, Austin, Texas, USA. Even earlier, I spent four amazing undergrad years at IIT Guwahati, India. I am a senior member of IEEE and have served as an editor on IEEE Transactions on Smart Grid.
